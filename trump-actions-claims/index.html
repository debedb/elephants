Claims under audit

Part of github.com/debedb/elephants — the sources, the rules, the argument and the case against it are all in the repository.

Thirty claims drawn at random from a third party's dataset of 3,466. For each one we go looking for the primary artifact behind it and report which clauses of the claim the artifact carries.

This page is 5 of 30 adjudicated. It is not a finished audit and must not be read as one. The remaining 25 rows are listed below, drawn but not yet worked. Every drawn row ships when it is done, including rows that come out fully carried and rows we abandon.

The draw was committed before any row was read, in its own commit, so that nothing can be quietly dropped for coming out inconveniently. The procedure was frozen before the draw.

What this page does not do

It does not decide whether claims are true. A clause our search did not support is a clause we could not source. That is a statement about our search and the reachable record, not about the world. You will not find a sentence here saying a claim is false.

It is not about the curator. A claim we could not source is not a finding of bad faith, error or incompetence by anyone. Character is out of scope in this repository and that applies here with extra force, because this page is pointed at one identifiable person's work — work that is larger in scope than anything on this site.

Thirty out of 3,466 is 0.9%. This page reports counts and never percentages. Nothing here measures the dataset.

The verdicts are computed, not written. Each row records four clause tests; a published function turns those into a verdict at render time. Our opinion is meant to sit in one rule you can read and re-run, not in thirty separate judgment calls. The two steelman paragraphs on each row are the only prose of ours doing any work, and both are required on every row.

Where this page stops

Our search is not exhaustive, and reachability turned out not to be a property of a document at all — it is a property of the tuple (document, fetcher, jurisdiction, moment). One row below is awaiting fetch for exactly that reason, with every route we tried named — a state rubric v2 added because v1 could not tell an artifact nobody can reach from one this fetcher cannot.
