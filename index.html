Elephants do not forget.

  • COVID-19, 2020–2021
    Orders, findings and vaccine authorizations on one axis. Each names the instrument behind it, who signed it and when — or says that none was ever published.
  • What the US government said about vaccines and transmission
    From "data are limited" to "you're not going to get COVID" to "vaccinated people can transmit Delta". Strictly .gov sources: FDA, CDC, MMWR and White House transcripts. No journalism, on any point.
  • Shoshin: a beginner's mind updates on the record
    Someone with no prior commitment reads both timelines in order and updates one belief per document. Reads the other pages live, so it changes when they change. Every weight is a slider — take the argument away from us.
